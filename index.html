Meet the author

Ahmed Hegazy

Ahmed is an Egyptian writer and tourism website designer, born in Bahariya Oasis in Giza Governorate. Growing up in one of Egypt's best-known desert destinations brought him into contact with travellers from an early age and shaped his love of adventure, travel and discovering Egypt.

A Software Engineering graduate of Ain Shams University, he combines practical tourism knowledge with technical experience building travel websites. Egypt Unfolded was created to help international visitors understand the country through useful, first-hand local context.
